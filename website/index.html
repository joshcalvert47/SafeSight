How it works

Four steps, all of them on your own machine.

01 Observe

SafeSight watches for images as pages render, including new nodes, lazy loads and background images.

02 Extract

Each image is drawn to a 224x224 canvas and reduced to raw RGB pixels. Cross-origin images go through an extension proxy.

03 Infer

The model returns five class probabilities, combined into a single 0 to 10 score.

04 Act

Below your threshold: revealed. Above it: blurred before first paint.

Private by architecture

The design makes it impossible for the sensitive parts to leave.

  • No image ever leaves your device. There is no upload path to disable.
  • The account stores your email, device name and counters. Nothing else.
  • The VPN handles only DNS. Web traffic never touches SafeSight.
  • Every verdict is a number on a 0 to 10 scale, against a threshold you chose.

Questions, answered

Do my images ever leave my device?

No. The model runs locally, in an offscreen worker in the extensions and natively in the Android app. Image bytes are scored and discarded inside the same process.

How is the score calculated?

The model outputs five class probabilities: neutral, drawing, hentai, porn and sexy. SafeSight combines the explicit classes into a weighted 0 to 10 score. Your sensitivity, from 1 to 9, is the threshold.

How many devices can I use?

Every account includes one device, guarded by your PIN. Additional devices can be added at any time as a paid add-on.

Will it slow down my browsing?

Analysis runs in a background worker with a small concurrency budget. Verdicts are cached per image URL and shared across duplicate images.

Can the Android firewall be bypassed?

Apps using system DNS are covered. Apps that hard-code their own resolver or use DNS-over-HTTPS can escape it, an accepted limitation of any DNS-based filter, disclosed openly.

What stops someone from turning it off?

Every protective change requires your account PIN, checked server-side with lockout after repeated wrong attempts.
